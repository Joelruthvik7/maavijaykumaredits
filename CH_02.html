01 / EARLY INCEPTION

Visual Media & Early Foundation

Chinthala Vijay Kumar developed his passion for filmmaking through an early immersion in visual media. Rather than observing motion as passive entertainment, his focus was drawn to composition, light dynamics, and the spatial tension within each individual shot.

02 / FORMAL CRAFT TRAINING

Government Film and Television Institute (2016–2019)

From 2016 to 2019, he underwent formal training in Cinematography. Here, the optical mechanics of lenses, lighting ratios, camera movement, and frame composition were cemented as his core instinct.

03 / ACTIVE PRODUCTION DIRECTION

Tamada Media (2019–2021)

Stepping directly onto sets as an Assistant and Director of Photography (DOP), he operated on live productions including Warangal Vandhana, Surya Kantham, Mr. Girlfriend, and Sathyabhama, gaining practical field mastery over framing, rhythm, and actor blocking.

04 / THE POST-PRODUCTION SUITE

Infinitum Network Solutions (2021–Present)

Transitioning his camera intuition into post-production, he serves as a Video Editor shaping high-engagement digital storytelling for prominent creators, translating raw camera takes into cohesive, emotionally tuned narratives.
